v2.2.3: Automation Map visualizes how your installed Hubitat apps and devices relate to each other, showing which app owns which device, what triggers or constrains a rule, and how everything connects, as an interactive graph you can explore, search, and export.

Since the last production release (2.2.1):

- Focusing an app, device or variable now fills the available space properly, instead of leaving a handful of nodes too small to read.
- The map no longer draws underneath the legend, the controls menu, or an open panel such as the rule flowchart, External systems or Pivot tables.
- Node labels are no longer cut off at the edge of the view.
- The map no longer visibly jumps about while a focused view settles.
