v2.3.2: Automation Map visualizes how your installed Hubitat apps and devices relate to each other, showing which app owns which device, what triggers or constrains a rule, and how everything connects, as an interactive graph you can explore, search, and export.

Since the last production release (2.3.1):

- Right-click any device or app on the map to open its page on the hub in a second tab. Devices go to the device page; apps and rules offer the read-only status page or the app page. The same menu copies a link to it, focuses it on the map, and re-reads it from the hub. Right-clicking empty space, or holding shift, still gives you the browser's own menu.
- Rescan this object re-reads one device or app without a full scan and updates the map in place, keeping your current view. A device is re-read through the apps that use it. Device labels, types and the Disabled flag are refreshed at the same time, so a device you disabled since the last scan now shows correctly.
- A short card explains the right-click the first time you open an object. "Opening objects on the hub" in the Focus panel brings it back whenever you want it.
- The AI friendly export gains two things (schema 14): a flag marking a condition that nothing evaluates, so a device that appears to gate a rule but does not is now visible to whatever reads the file, and a per-piston webCoRE migration rating for Rule Machine and Visual Rule Builder 2.0 with the reasons behind it. Ratings come from the Migration Assessment panel, so open it once and the export picks them up.
